How to use
  • From Primers (easiest): paste primers you already designed from the other PEEK tabs — the viewer will find where they bind and draw them on the sequence.
  • Silent Mutation: copy the codon region you want to change as the original window, paste the synonymous version as the mutated window.
  • Insertion: pick the position (1-based) where you want to insert extra DNA or amino acids.
  • Primers are designed using Q5 polymerase / NEB rules targeting 60-70 °C.
  • Highlighted bases show where each primer binds (blue = forward, red = reverse) and where changes occur (amber).
