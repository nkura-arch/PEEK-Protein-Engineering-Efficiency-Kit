Scoring Methodology
Codon Adaptation Index (CAI)

Measures how well the codon usage matches the host organism's preferences. Higher values (closer to 1.0) indicate better adaptation and potentially higher expression.

Rare Codon Clusters

Consecutive rare codons can cause ribosome stalling and reduce translation efficiency. Fewer clusters is better.

GC Content

Optimal range is typically 40-60%. Extreme values can affect mRNA stability and expression.

Composite Score

Combines all metrics: Score = CAI×100 - RareClusters×5 - Changes×2 - Homopolymers×3 + SiteBonus
